## UG Admission

| Department                              | Annual Intake  |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Artificial Intelligence & Data Science  | 60             |
| Civil Engineering                       | 60             |
| Computer Science & Engineering          | 120            |
| Computer Science & Engineering (AIML)   | 60             |
| Electronics & Communication Engineering | 60             |
| Mechanical Engineering                  | 60             |
| **Total**                               | **420 / year** |

**Eligibility:** 2nd PUC / 12th / equivalent, English + PCM (or equivalent
stream subject), minimum 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/Cat-1/2A/2B/3A/3B).
Qualifying rank in CET or COMEDK required.

**Admission routes:**

| Quota      | Authority                                                               | Eligible candidates                |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| CET        | Karnataka Examination Authority (kea.kar.nic.in)                        | Karnataka domicile only            |
| COMEDK     | Karnataka Unaided Private Engineering Colleges Association (comedk.org) | Karnataka + non-Karnataka          |
| Management | Direct application to MIT + counselling                                 | Anyone meeting minimum eligibility |
